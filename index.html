Privacy Policy

The Yuka Community Ireland · “Yuka Poster” app · Last updated 16 November 2026

The Yuka Community Ireland is an independent community Facebook page for people in Ireland who use the Yuka food and cosmetics scanning app. This policy covers Yuka Poster, a small Facebook app that the page’s administrator uses to publish posts to the page.

What the app does

Yuka Poster is used only by the page administrator. It publishes prepared posts and photos to The Yuka Community Ireland Facebook page. Members of the public do not log in to it, install it or interact with it directly.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or process personal information about people who view, like, comment on or share the page’s posts.

The only information it handles is:

  • the page administrator’s Facebook access token, which lets the app post to the page. It is stored securely as an encrypted secret and is never shared.
  • the text and photos of the posts being published.

How information is used

The access token is used only to publish posts and photo credits to The Yuka Community Ireland page. It is not used to read your profile, messages or friends, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Photos come from Unsplash and are credited in a comment on each post.

Facebook

Your comments, reactions and messages on the page are handled by Facebook under Meta’s Privacy Policy. The page administrator can see them in the same way as on any Facebook page, but the app does not collect or store them.

Data deletion

Yuka Poster stores no personal data about page visitors, so there is nothing to delete for them.

If you have connected to the app in any way and want that connection removed, you can do it yourself in Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find “Yuka Poster” and choose Remove.

You can also send a message to The Yuka Community Ireland page on Facebook asking for any data to be deleted. We will confirm once it is done.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, send a message to The Yuka Community Ireland page on Facebook.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

The Yuka Community Ireland is an independent fan community. It is not run by, affiliated with or endorsed by Yuka SAS, the makers of the Yuka app.
